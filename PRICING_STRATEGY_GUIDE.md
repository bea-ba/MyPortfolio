## 🧠 Psychology Perspective: My Original Strategy Evaluation

### My Initial Instinct (Nov 2025)
"Learning phase ends when I get organic gigs regularly filling my available time"

### ⚠️ Psychological Concerns with This Approach

#### 1. External Validation Trap
- **Risk:** Waiting for market to "tell me" I'm ready
- **Problem:** Amplifies imposter syndrome ("Maybe I'm not really *that* good yet...")
- **Result:** Moving goalposts—when busy, I might still feel "not ready"

#### 2. Reactive Identity Formation
- **Risk:** Letting clients define my worth vs. owning my value
- **Problem:** No internal compass for readiness
- **Result:** Constant uncertainty about when to level up

#### 3. Dangerous "Filling Time" Language
- **Risk:** Reacting to demand rather than designing my practice
- **Problem:** No natural stopping point—when is "full" actually full?
- **Result:** Hit capacity at rates too low to sustain → burnout

#### 4. Busyness ≠ Readiness
- **Risk:** Getting busy with the WRONG kind of work
- **Problem:** Psychology research shows people mistake "busyness" for "progress"
- **Result:** Trapped in high-volume, low-value work

## 💼 Business/Career Coach Perspective

### What's Smart About My Thinking
✅ Market validation matters
✅ Not arbitrarily picking dates
✅ Understanding capacity as a signal

### 🚨 Strategic Problem with "Wait Until Full" Approach

**Critical Insight:** Raise prices BEFORE at capacity, not after

**Why this matters:**
- At 70-80% capacity: Raising prices filters for better clients, creates breathing room
- At 100% capacity: No time to pursue higher-value opportunities
- When overbooked at low rates: TRAPPED—can't take better work, can't raise rates on existing clients

### The Timeline Reality
- **Months 1-3:** Take any client to learn
- **Months 4-6:** Know which projects energize vs. drain
- **Months 7-9:** Have portfolio pieces + testimonials
- **Months 10-12:** Clear sense of value

### Trap Scenario (What to Avoid)
- Month 3: "Getting traction! Keep rates low to maintain momentum..."
- Month 6: "Fully booked! But clients found me at these rates, can't raise now..."
- Month 9: "Burnt out and underpaid, but no time to find better clients..."
- Month 12: "Freelancing doesn't pay enough, quitting..."

### Success Scenario (What to Aim For)
- Month 3: "Learned a ton. Raising rates 20% for new clients next month."
- Month 6: "At 70% capacity. Raising rates another 20%."
- Month 9: "Have a waitlist. Rates reflect my experience now."
- Month 12: "Sustainable practice with clients I love at rates that respect my value."

## 🎯 Decision Framework for Rate Increases

### When to Raise Rates (ANY of these signals):

1. **Capacity Signal:** At 70%+ of available time with current clients
2. **Confidence Signal:** Solved 3+ problems that initially intimidated me
3. **Quality Signal:** Turning down work because it's not aligned (not because of time)
4. **Referral Signal:** Getting organic inquiries from past client recommendations
5. **Timeline Signal:** Reached scheduled quarterly review date

### How Much to Raise

**Conservative Approach (Recommended for first year):**
- 15-20% every 3 months for NEW clients only
- Grandfathered rates for existing ongoing projects

**Aggressive Approach (If demand is very high):**
- 25-30% increases
- Communicate clearly to existing clients if applying retroactively

### What to Update When Raising Rates

- [ ] Update FAQ on `/intake` page with new rates
- [ ] Update "rates valid through [date]" timeline
- [ ] Update internal rate sheet for consistency
- [ ] Update proposal templates
- [ ] Communicate to any waiting list contacts

## 🚧 Common Traps & How to Avoid Them

### Trap #1: "I'm too busy to raise rates"
**Reality:** Being busy at low rates is THE signal to raise prices
**Action:** Raise rates when at 70% capacity, not 100%

### Trap #2: "But I'm not an expert yet"
**Reality:** Clients pay for problem-solving, not credentials
**Action:** Price based on value delivered, not years of experience

### Trap #3: "I'll lose clients if I raise rates"
**Reality:** Wrong clients will leave, right clients will stay
**Action:** You WANT to filter for people who value your work appropriately

### Trap #4: "I need more portfolio pieces first"
**Reality:** Moving goalposts—there's always "one more thing"
**Action:** If you've completed 3-5 projects successfully, you're ready to increase

### Trap #5: "Market conditions aren't right"
**Reality:** There's never a "perfect" time
**Action:** Stick to quarterly schedule regardless of external factors
